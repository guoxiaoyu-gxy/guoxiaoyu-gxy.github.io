## Research Profile

AI researcher with a PhD in Artificial Intelligence from Monash University and research experience spanning academia and industry. My research focuses on natural language processing, large language models, complex reasoning, multimodal learning, and responsible AI, with publications in leading international conferences, including EMNLP and COLING. I have a strong track record of developing novel machine learning methods, constructing evaluation benchmarks, and investigating model robustness, fairness, and generalisation. My experience includes conducting independent and collaborative research, supervising research students, and translating research outcomes into practical AI applications. I am particularly interested in advancing trustworthy, efficient, and generalisable AI systems.
