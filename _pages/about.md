biography: |
  I am an AI researcher with experience across academia and industry. My work focuses on natural language processing, large language models, complex reasoning, multimodal learning, and responsible AI. I am particularly interested in building trustworthy, efficient, and generalisable AI systems.

  Previously, I worked as a Senior Applied Scientist at [Oracle Australia](https://www.oracle.com/au/) (January 2025–May 2026), developing medical-domain agent workflows, scalable AI solutions, and methods for model evaluation and validation. Before that, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the [Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML)](https://www.adelaide.edu.au/aiml/) (November 2023–January 2025), working with [Ehsan Abbasnejad](https://ehsanabb.github.io/) on model robustness, reliability, and responsible AI.

  I completed my PhD in Artificial Intelligence at [Monash University](https://www.monash.edu/) in April 2024, under the supervision of [Yuan-Fang Li](https://liyuanfang.github.io/) and [Gholamreza Haffari](https://rezahaffari.github.io/HomePage/HomePage.html). My thesis, [*Complex Question Answering: from Data to Method*](https://bridges.monash.edu/articles/thesis/Complex_Question_Answering_from_Data_to_Method/25428691), explored reasoning with neural module networks and language models under data bias and distribution challenges.

## Research Interests
Natural language processing and large language models; complex question answering and reasoning; multimodal learning; model robustness, fairness, and generalisation; responsible AI.
